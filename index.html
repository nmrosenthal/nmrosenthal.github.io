About

I turn satellite and climate data into decisions about risk: what could happen to a place, what it would cost, and how to transfer that risk.

I built a wildfire catastrophe model and designed parametric (re)insurance triggers that grew to more than $10M in gross written premium. Today I lead go-to-market for a geospatial foundation model platform at LGND AI. I hold a PhD from UCLA, where I studied how wildfire smoke and extreme heat combine to harm health, and I teach remote sensing at New York University.

Climate risk

Wildfire, flood, heat and smoke, measured from space and modeled for the people who carry the loss.

Risk transfer

Catastrophe models, parametric triggers and basis risk for insurers and reinsurers.

Geospatial AI

Foundation models and cloud-native Earth data, taken from pilot to product.
