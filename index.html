About

I work where Earth observation meets the balance sheet: what could happen to a place, what it would cost, and who should carry that risk.

I built a wildfire catastrophe model and designed parametric triggers for (re)insurance products that grew past $10M in gross written premium. Today I lead go-to-market for a geospatial foundation model platform at LGND AI. My PhD at UCLA studied how wildfire smoke and extreme heat combine to harm health, and I teach remote sensing at New York University.

Climate risk
